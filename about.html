About Sweet Crumb Bakery

Sweet Crumb Bakery is a small neighbourhood bakery. We bake bread, croissants, pastries and cakes fresh every morning for families, students and office teams.

Our goal is simple: good ingredients, honest recipes and a warm place to pick up something sweet.
